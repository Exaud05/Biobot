What is CO-RE II Technology?
The second generation of our exclusive Compressed O-Ring Expansion (CO-RE II) technology starts with a special O-ring on the coupling of each pipetting channel in our automated liquid handlers. This O-ring precisely corresponds with a small circular groove on the interior of our CO-RE II pipette tips and CO-RE tools.
The O-ring is compressed when a tip or tool is attached to a pipette channel to create an air-tight seal without the use of mechanical force.
Compared to our previous generation technology, CO-RE II offers these benefits:
Extended tip sealing life of O-rings, up to 15x the average life span
Lifetime stop disk design
Enhanced hardware chemical resistance
Improved tip alignment and retention
Extended service interval requirements
Equivalent fluidic performance on existing methods with no change in TADM or liquid class
Reduced service needs
