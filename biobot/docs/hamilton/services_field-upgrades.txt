Keeping Your Automation Future-Ready
Hamilton’s Field Upgrade services allow your Hamilton automated liquid handler to adapt to your evolving requirements. This service is currently available for our
Microlab STAR Line
solutions.
Whether you seek to enhance walkaway time, increase sample throughput, or automate additional assays, our factory-certified Field Service Engineers can reconfigure and upgrade your hardware quickly and easily. Most changes may be made in your lab within one to two days to minimize downtime and maximize the value of your automated liquid handler investment.

Optimize, Expand, and Enhance
Hamilton Field Service Engineers are equipped to make many changes to your Microlab STAR Line automated liquid handler. Examples include:
Devices such as a vacuum station, heaters, coolers, and shakers
Up to 8 x 5 mL CO-RE II pipetting channels
Up to 16 x 1 mL CO-RE II pipetting channels
CO-RE Multi-Probe Heads (96 and 384)
Labware handling tools (CO-RE Gripper, iSWAP, Tube Gripper Channel)
Imaging channel with USB camera
Single-arm instrument upgrade to dual-arm instrument
