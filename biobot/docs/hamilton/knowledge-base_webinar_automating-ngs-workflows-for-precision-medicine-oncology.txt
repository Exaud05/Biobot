In this Webinar, Dr. Elisabeth Maritschnegg, Product Manager Oncology at CeGaT, will describe the drivers behind the need for automating CeGaT's NGS workflows and the benefits of their current automated systems. Moreover, she will illustrate the benefits of CeGaT's genomic services through two case studies.
At the conclusion of the Webinar, Dr. Gabriela Boza-Moran, Scientific Content Manager at Hamilton Robotics, presents some of the commercial solutions that Hamilton Robotics can provide to customers using NGS in Precision Medicine-Oncology Studies.
