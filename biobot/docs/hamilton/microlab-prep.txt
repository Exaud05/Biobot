Hamilton Microlab Prep
Your Lab’s Ideal Benchtop Automated Liquid Handler
Take your first step into automation with time-tested Hamilton quality packed into an affordable liquid handler. Since our founding more than 70 years ago, Hamilton has been dedicated to developing the highest quality automated liquid handling platforms to support scientific research across the globe.
The Hamilton Microlab Prep packs our signature quality and efficiency into a small liquid handler workstation that easily fits on a lab bench or in many biological safety cabinets. The Microlab Prep is the solution to make your workflows faster, more precise, and more convenient than ever. Get ready to revolutionize the work in your lab by replacing manual pipetting with the efficiency and accuracy of the Microlab Prep.

Pipetting Technology
CO-RE II Tip/Tool Attachment
The Key to Liquid Handling Workflow Excellence
The new CO-RE II technology assures more precise tip alignment, minimizing the risk of collisions. Also, tip coupling is faster which boosts productivity. Besides tips, a wide range of Hamilton tools are applicable with CO-RE II.
Transport Solutions
CO-RE Gripper
For Additional Movements
Further streamline automated workflows with CO-RE Grippers. These agile grippers transform Hamilton's precision pipette channels into on-deck labware transport solutions on Hamilton automated liquid handlers.
Software
Graphical User Interface
For Easy Operation
The intuitive graphical user interface (GUI) makes operation as simple as a smartphone app. Get started developing, editing, and running protocols without the need for automation or coding expertise.
Machine Vision
Labware Detection and Verification
For Fast Protocol Development
A camera mounted on top of the workstation detects and identifies labware placed on the deck. This visual recognition simplifies the process of programming new protocols by eliminating the need to scroll through long lists of options. It also ensures accurate verification of labware during protocol execution, helping to prevent loading errors.

How do I replace legacy CO-RE stop discs and o-rings on the Microlab Prep?
When one of the 2 Independent Channels or the 8-Probe Head reaches 20,000 tip eject cycles, the Microlab Prep will recommend replacing its stop disc(s) and o-ring(s). This article describes how to perform this maintenance routine.
