Join Hamilton’s experts for a two-part webinar series exploring innovative solutions designed to optimize automated blood fractionation workflows. In Part 1, taking place on November 27th, 2025, we’ll take a deep dive into two cutting-edge technologies: easyBlood, powered by advanced imaging and pipetting technology, and Liquid Fractionation Control (LFC), which leverages Hamilton’s proprietary Total Aspiration and Dispense Monitoring (TADM) technology for pressure-based liquid-liquid interface detection. Discover how these technologies enable precise separation of plasma, buffy coat, and red blood cells while ensuring full process traceability and sample integrity.
In Part 2, scheduled for early 2026, we’ll present a real-world field study comparing these two methods side-by-side in an applied laboratory setting, demonstrating their performance and practical benefits under real conditions.
This webinar series is ideal for diagnostic and research laboratories, biobanks, and anyone looking to enhance sample preparation efficiency and reliability in blood-based assays.
